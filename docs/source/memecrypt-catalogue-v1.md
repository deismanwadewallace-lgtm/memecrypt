# Memecrypt Gift Shop — Catalogue Draft v1

Ten objects. Each entry carries an accession number, a wall label in museum voice, a provenance line, and shop copy for the buy button. Prices are USD placeholders.

Accession format: **MC.2026.###**. The number is permanent. Sold-out objects stay in the catalogue and read "no longer in circulation."

---

## MC.2026.001 — Specimen Tag

**Object.** Hard enamel pin, 32 mm, brass plating, rubber clutch. Stamped edition of 1,000.

**Wall label.**
Specimen tags were once tied to the toe of an object, not a body. The tag outlived the specimen in most collections. Curators kept the number and lost the thing. This is the standard Memecrypt tag: catalogue number, classification, and a provenance field that has never once been filled in honestly.

**Provenance.** Origin unknown. Acquired from a group chat. No prior owner has come forward.

**Shop copy.** Wear the number, not the meme. $14.

## MC.2026.014 — Deaccession Stamp

**Object.** Wood-handled rubber stamp, 60 x 20 mm impression. Black archival ink pad included.

**Wall label.**
Deaccessioning is the formal removal of an object from a permanent collection. Museums do it quietly, in writing, with a committee. The internet does it never. This stamp restores the missing procedure to the general public.

**Provenance.** Manufactured 2026. Intended use: laptops, notebooks, the back of anyone's hand, printed screenshots of arguments you have already won.

**Shop copy.** Retire it formally. $26.

## MC.2026.031 — Field Guide to Zombie Memes

**Object.** Saddle-stitched pocket guide, 4.5 x 7 in, 96 pages, two-colour interior, 40 species plates.

**Wall label.**
Organized by behaviour, not nostalgia. Each species entry gives habitat, diet, predators, and undead rating on a five-skull scale. The guide names no real person and reproduces no photograph. Species are described the way a birder describes a bird, which turns out to be the correct register for this material.

**Sample entry.** *Reactio perpetua*, the Perpetual Reaction. Habitat: reply chains, quote-tweets, any thread past forty comments. Diet: sincerity. Predators: none observed. Undead rating: four skulls. Notes: does not require the original post to survive. Has been documented feeding on posts that were deleted years earlier.

**Shop copy.** Identification in the field. $22.

## Notes on the set

**Price ladder.** $12 to $180. The tin and the pin are impulse. The field guide and the plate are the volume sellers. The anti-meme plate exists to make the whole shop legible as an argument, and it should never be discounted.

**Reuse.** Cause of death, species entry, and wall label are all formats, not one-offs. Each generates unlimited SKUs at near-zero design cost.

**Copyright.** Nothing here reproduces a photograph, a character, or a real person. Every image is commissioned or absent.
