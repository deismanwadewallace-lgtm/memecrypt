# Image-generation prompts

The three storefront mockups were created with the built-in image-generation tool. These are the exact final prompts.

## Specimen 001 tee

```text
Use case: product-mockup
Asset type: square e-commerce catalog image for the Memecrypt museum gift shop
Primary request: premium black heavyweight T-shirt photographed as a real retail product, printed with a stark museum specimen label for an internet zombie idea
Scene/backdrop: nearly black studio background with an extremely faint archival grid
Subject: single black T-shirt, front facing, laid flat with natural fabric folds
Style/medium: photorealistic premium apparel product photography with precise screen-printed graphic
Composition/framing: centered square image, generous clean padding, shirt fully visible
Lighting/mood: controlled softbox lighting, forensic museum mood, subtle edge light
Color palette: carbon black, bone white, acid yellow-green #B9D52B
Materials/textures: heavyweight washed cotton, matte ink, realistic seams and folds
Text (verbatim): "SPECIMEN 001" and "ZOMBIE IDEA" and "OUTBREAK LEVEL: HIGH"
Typography: condensed bold uppercase grotesk; compact museum evidence-label hierarchy; render each phrase exactly once
Constraints: original design; no people; no hanger; no other objects; no extra text; no unrelated symbols; no logos besides a tiny original acid-green skull specimen icon; no watermark
Avoid: horror gore, Halloween styling, distressed novelty-shirt clichés, neon glow
```

## Zombie Idea Field Guide

```text
Use case: product-mockup
Asset type: square e-commerce catalog image for the Memecrypt museum gift shop
Primary request: a museum field-guide card deck called "ZOMBIE IDEA FIELD GUIDE", designed as an educational identification toolkit for persistent internet ideas
Scene/backdrop: matte black archival tabletop with a faint specimen grid
Subject: one compact black card box standing upright with five cards fanned beside it; each card uses simple original diagrams: habitat, vector, host, mutation, and undead rating
Style/medium: premium photorealistic product photography; Swiss-modern museum graphics; crisp printed packaging
Composition/framing: centered three-quarter product arrangement in a square frame with clean breathing room
Lighting/mood: precise forensic tabletop lighting, intelligent and slightly ominous
Color palette: carbon black, bone white, acid yellow-green #B9D52B, tiny gray rules
Materials/textures: uncoated black paper, matte laminate, crisp card stock, subtle edge wear only
Text (verbatim): "ZOMBIE IDEA FIELD GUIDE" and "IDENTIFY BEFORE YOU AMPLIFY"
Typography: condensed bold uppercase grotesk with monospaced micro-label accents; render both phrases exactly once and legibly
Constraints: original packaging; no people; no hands; no gore; no unrelated props; no extra words; no existing logos or trademarks; no watermark
Avoid: fantasy tarot imagery, playing-card suits, fluorescent glow, comic-book zombies
```

## Context Consumes poster

```text
Use case: product-mockup
Asset type: square e-commerce catalog image for the Memecrypt museum gift shop
Primary request: a limited-edition museum exhibition poster about context collapse and the persistence of memes
Scene/backdrop: nearly black gallery wall and black tabletop edge, minimal and architectural
Subject: single unframed portrait poster leaning slightly against the wall; bold typographic archival design with one small original acid-green skull specimen icon and faint crop/compression artifacts as abstract texture
Style/medium: photorealistic gallery product photography; austere modern museum poster design; crisp ink on heavy off-white stock
Composition/framing: centered square catalog crop with entire poster visible and generous negative space
Lighting/mood: soft raking gallery light, sober, intelligent, forensic
Color palette: carbon black, warm bone white, acid yellow-green #B9D52B, faint neutral gray
Materials/textures: thick uncoated paper, subtle tooth, sharp printed typography
Text (verbatim): "CONTEXT IS THE FIRST THING A MEME CONSUMES."
Typography: large condensed bold uppercase grotesk, exact punctuation, phrase rendered exactly once
Constraints: no frame; no people; no hands; no gore; no extra text; no existing logos or trademarks; no watermark
Avoid: Halloween visuals, cartoon zombies, neon glow, vintage propaganda pastiche
```
