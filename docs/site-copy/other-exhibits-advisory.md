# Copy for `/museum/other-exhibits`

Implements ADR 0003: the museum declines to display; it is not awaiting
clearance.

## Standfirst

Fourteen influential specimens, catalogued by name and held behind the rights
register. Each line-art mark records a decision, not a gap. Nothing here is
unfinished.

## Display advisory

**Display advisory**

The museum declines to display these photographs. It is not waiting for
permission.

Ten of these fourteen specimens could be shown under the museum's own policy,
at thumbnail scale, with commentary and attribution. The museum has chosen not
to. Four cannot be shown on any terms.

Do not add the photographs. A specimen leaves this treatment only by a recorded
curatorial decision, never by upload.

## Per-specimen status labels

- `declined` → **Declined for display**
- `unavailable` → **Not permitted for display**
- Register row missing → **Not yet registered**

"Pending" is retired from the vocabulary. It described a queue that does not
exist.
