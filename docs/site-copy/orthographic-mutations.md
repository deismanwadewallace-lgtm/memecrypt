Department of Orthographic Mutations

# Language caught in the act of becoming contagious.

Sometimes a meme needs a photograph. Sometimes it needs four letters, one
missing verb, or a comma in mortal danger.

This collection isolates twelve mutations in written language: errors repeated
until they became conventions, rules turned into rival camps, and typographic
accidents that acquired a voice. The images have been removed from the
experiment. What remains is the mechanism.

**12 provisional specimens · 0 third-party images · 5 writers’ field notes**

> A typo is an accident. A meme begins when someone learns how to repeat it.

## Collection note

The labels `OM-01` through `OM-12` are working research identifiers. They do
not place these objects in the permanent `M##` register. Source links establish

character, or screenshot.
